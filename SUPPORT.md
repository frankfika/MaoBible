# Support

MaoBible is a personal project maintained by Frank Chen. Support is
best-effort and free of charge.

## Contact

- General support: <support@maobible.app>
- Privacy / data requests: <privacy@maobible.app>
- Source: <https://github.com/frankfika/maobible>
- Issue tracker: <https://github.com/frankfika/maobible/issues>

## Response time

This is a side project maintained outside business hours. Expect a reply
within 1–3 working days; security and data-deletion requests are
prioritized.

## How to file a good bug report

1. Device + OS version (e.g. iPhone 13, iOS 17.4; Pixel 7, Android 14).
2. App version (Settings → Me → 隐私政策 → 顶部版本号).
3. PWA vs. native build (was the app installed from the App Store /
   Play Store, or used as an installed PWA from the website?).
4. Reproduction steps: what you tapped, what you expected, what
   happened instead. Screenshots help.
5. If the bug involves a specific article, include the article id
   (visible in the URL: `/read/{id}`).

## Data requests

- **Access / export** of your reading data: open the app, go to
  Me → 隐私政策 → 数据删除. (Direct export is on the roadmap — see
  CHANGELOG for status.)
- **Deletion** of all data: same path → "清空本机全部数据". Uninstalling
  the app also wipes all local data.

## Out of scope

- 中文文本 (人民出版社 1991) 出版权咨询 — please contact 人民出版社
  directly.
- 英文翻译校对 — `translations.en` is currently `status: draft` and
  not under a commercial translation agreement.
- App Store / Play Store listing / promotion assistance.
